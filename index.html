ShadowBox 6×6 Student Generator

Students upload a clean black silhouette. The app creates the standard 6×6×6-inch design and generates STL files for the base, four rim walls, and light holder. Processing happens locally in the browser.

Student submission

Upload a silhouette to begin.

Student image: solid black subject on white or transparent background. Keep the subject away from the left/right edges. The app extracts the upper contour and converts it into the rim profile.

Prototype optics: centered point-like LED, 1.00 in above the bottom, maximum light diameter 0.25 in. Projection distance is measured from the outside wall to the receiving surface.
